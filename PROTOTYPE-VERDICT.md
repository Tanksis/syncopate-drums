# Prototype verdict: entering note marks (2026-10-26)

Question: how does a drummer enter accents, flams/drags and roll strokes (double, buzz) in the editor?

Variants (`?variant=` on the Practice screen, switcher at the bottom):
- A: pick a note in the notation; a popover of marks, keys > F D / Z.
- B: a tool palette over the beat cards (Hit, Accent, Flam, Drag, Double, Buzz); tapping a cell applies the tool, adding the hit if empty.
- C: three mark lanes over each card's snare row (accent, grace, roll), tap to cycle.

**Verdict: B, the tool palette.** The user found A's popover janky to deal with. C was not chosen.

The user also said the rendering of the marks needs work. Seen in a close-up:
- the flam grace note is too big and too far from its note;
- the drag grace notes look like real 16ths (too big, inside the beam, with a slur);
- the double stroke draws as a block at the stem tip instead of a slash across the stem;
- the buzz Z sits on the notehead instead of across the stem;
- the accents float high above the stems;
- the grace notes' hands (lR, llR) aren't printed.
