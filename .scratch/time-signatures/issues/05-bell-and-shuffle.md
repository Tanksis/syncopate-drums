# 05: Bell in 6/8 and Shuffle in 12/8

**What to build:** Two compound patterns. **Bell** (Afro-Cuban, 6/8) plays `x.xxx.` on the ride bell, written as an x on the ride's line. **Shuffle** (Blues, 12/8) plays the ride on the first and third eighth of each beat (`x.xx.xx.xx.x`). They are listed only in their own meters. See [spec](../spec.md), story 21, and Implementation Decisions › Patterns.
