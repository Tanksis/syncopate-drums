# 10: Help and About

**What to build:** A Help page, opened from a **?** in the desktop header and **Help** in the phone's ☰ menu. It covers a three-step quick start (make an exercise, enter hits on the grid, press play), the keys (reuse `keyHelp.ts` and the Metronome's keys), and short definitions of exercise, bar, snare row, kick row, sticking, groove, hi-hat foot, swing, loop and count-in, worded from `CONTEXT.md`. An About section says what Syncopate! is, that exercises stay on this device, how to export them, and the open-source credits (VexFlow and the rest of `package.json`'s runtime dependencies). See [spec](../spec.md), stories 23–25.
